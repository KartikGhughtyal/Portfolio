07 — Method

How I Solve Problems

01

Understand

Read carefully, restate the problem, identify inputs/outputs and constraints.

02

Write the simplest working solution first. Confirm correctness on examples.

03

Identify bottlenecks. Apply the right data structure or algorithm.

04

Analyze

Compute time & space complexity. Justify the trade-offs.
